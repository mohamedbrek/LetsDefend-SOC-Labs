# LetsDefend SOC Labs

Conducting SOC labs and investigations through LetsDefend.

## Overview

This repository hosts the practical SOC investigations and lab reports conducted on LetsDefend where I have been learning and gaining hands-on experience in cybersecurity and security operations.


## Lab Categories


- **Phishing Analysis** – Looking at suspicious emails and phishing alerts.
- **Malware Analysis** – Assessing malware activity and points of compromise.
- **Threat hunting** – Detecting suspicious activity and threats.
- **Alert Triage** – Evaluating security alerts and classifying their severity.
- **Incident Response** – Writing down the results of my work.


## Skills Developed


- Investigation and triage of security alerts
- Log and event analysis
- Threat identification
- Indicators of Compromise (IOC) analysis
- Incident reporting

Repository Structure

Each folder contains an individual lab including relevant investigation, findings, screenshots and reports where applicable.
